# FEARLESS Bot Update - May 14, 2026

## ✨ New Features
- **Bleeding Hellish Heart:** A rare and powerful heart has been spotted! Claiming it yields a massive amount of Bloodern. It appears every few hours.

## 🚀 Improvements
- **Vendor Standardization:** The Shop and Merchant have moved! All automated spawns now occur exclusively in the General channel for easier tracking.
- **Enhanced Potions:** Potions of Fearless and Ruthless have received a major potency boost. They now last for 2 full hours.
- **Persistent Menus:** The `/help` and `/changelog` menus are now permanent. They will no longer time out or stop working.
- **Merchant Gorelith Stock:** The Merchant has refined his trade. Gorelith is now available as a limited stock item rather than a simple button conversion.
- **UI Refresh:** All menu buttons have been standardized for a sleeker look and intuitive navigation.
- **Role Notifications:** Notifications for special devices have been updated for better accuracy.

## 🛠️ Bug Fixes
- **Menu Stability:** Resolved an issue where clicking buttons in the `/changelog` menu would occasionally cause the bot to error.
- **Navigation Fix:** Improved the paging logic in the `/help` menu for smoother browsing.

## ⚠️ Removal
- **Manual Conversion:** The manual conversion button has been removed from the Merchant in favor of the new stock system.
